# Setting up GenesisGradeLens on a Chromebook

> **Status:** draft. Student sign-in support is not finished yet. These steps install the app,
> but until student support ships it only works when a **parent** is signed in to Genesis.

Takes about 5 minutes. You only do steps 1–3 once.

## Before you start: is this your own Chromebook?

GenesisGradeLens is installed by hand ("Load unpacked"). **School-issued Chromebooks usually
block this.** If you see either of these in step 2, stop. It won't work on that Chromebook, and
you should use a home computer instead:

- The **Developer mode** switch is grayed out.
- A message says the setting or extension is **blocked by your administrator**.

Don't try to get around the block. It's the school's device and the school's rules.

## 1. Get the app

1. Your parent sends you **GenesisGradeLens.zip** (by email or Google Drive). Save it to your
   **Downloads** folder.
2. Open the **Files** app and go to **Downloads**.
3. Double-click **GenesisGradeLens.zip**. It opens like a folder.
4. Inside, right-click the **GenesisGradeLens** folder → **Copy**.
5. Go back to **Downloads**, right-click an empty spot → **Paste**.
   You now have a normal **GenesisGradeLens** folder in Downloads. Keep it there; Chrome
   needs it to stay put.

## 2. Add it to Chrome

1. Open Chrome and type `chrome://extensions` in the address bar, then press Enter.
2. Turn on **Developer mode** (switch at the top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked**.
4. Choose **Downloads → GenesisGradeLens** (the folder, not the zip) and click **Open**.
5. A **GenesisGradeLens** card appears. You can turn Developer mode back off now.

## 3. Pin the button

1. Click the **puzzle-piece** icon to the right of the address bar.
2. Find **GenesisGradeLens** and click the **pin** next to it.
   A blue magnifying-glass icon now stays in your toolbar.

## 4. Use it

1. In a Chrome tab, sign in to Genesis at **students.summit.k12.nj.us** as usual.
   (GenesisGradeLens never asks for your password. It uses the login you already have open.)
2. Click the blue magnifying-glass icon. Your courses appear.
3. Tap a course to see how its grade is built. Switch to **What if…** to try out scores on
   upcoming assignments.

If it says **"Sign in to Genesis"**, sign in in another tab, then press **I've signed in**.

## Updating to a new version

1. Delete the old **GenesisGradeLens** folder in Downloads and copy in the new one (step 1).
2. Go to `chrome://extensions` and click the **reload** arrow on the GenesisGradeLens card.

Your saved what-if scenarios stay as long as you don't click **Remove**.

## Removing it

`chrome://extensions` → GenesisGradeLens → **Remove**.
